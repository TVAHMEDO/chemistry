How to Practice

  1. Pick a chapter

    Choose an available chapter from the Chemistry list.

  2. Set your quiz

    Choose how many questions to attempt, from 1 up to the full question bank.

  3. Answer one by one

    Select an option, then press Check Answer to see if you are right.

  4. Move on

    Read the short explanation, then press Next Question.

  5. Review your result

    See your score, percentage and every answer with its explanation.

Marking: every MCQ carries 1 mark. There is no negative marking. An answer counts only after you press Check Answer, and it cannot be changed afterwards.
